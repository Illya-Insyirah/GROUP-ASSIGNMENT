Beauty Tips

💄 Makeup Tips for Long-Lasting

  • Start with clean & moisturized skin
  • Always use primer before makeup
  • Apply makeup in thin layers
  • Set with loose powder
  • Finish with setting spray

🎨 Tips to Choose the Right Makeup Shade

  • Know your skin undertone (Warm / Cool / Neutral)
  • Check veins on your wrist
  • Test foundation on jawline, not hand
  • Match shade under natural light
  • Choose formula based on skin type
